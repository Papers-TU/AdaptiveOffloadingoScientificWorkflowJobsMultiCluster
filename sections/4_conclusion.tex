We presented SOU, a Snakemake extension that offloads workflow jobs across clusters to meet a user-defined time-to-result by combining Bayesian runtime prediction with workflow simulation. On two bioinformatics workflows, SOU meets the target in five of six configurations with a makespan prediction error of 6.0\,\% and reduces cost by 40.1\,\% compared to naive offloading. Future work will re-evaluate offloading decisions at runtime~\cite{da_silva_online_2015, hilman_task_2018}, support heterogeneous clusters~\cite{bader_lotaru_2022, bader_lotaru_2024}, and include network, energy, and CO\textsubscript{2} metrics in the offloading criterion.
