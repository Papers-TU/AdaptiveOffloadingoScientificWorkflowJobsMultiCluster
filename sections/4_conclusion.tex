This paper presented SOU, a Snakemake extension that offloads workflow jobs across clusters by combining regression-based runtime prediction with workflow simulation to meet a user-defined time-to-result. The evaluation on two bioinformatics workflows demonstrated the feasibility of this approach. Future work will re-evaluate offloading decisions at runtime~\cite{da_silva_online_2015, hilman_task_2018}, support heterogeneous clusters~\cite{bader_lotaru_2024}, and include network, energy, and CO\textsubscript{2} metrics in the offloading criterion.
